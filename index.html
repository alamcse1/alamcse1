About

Cyber Security Specialist at KION Group since July 2022, working across security architecture, security engineering, monitoring and incident response. I own security infrastructure end to end — from SIEM and EDR platforms through to the identity provider — and design the detection rules and solutions that surface malicious behaviour so SOC analysts can act on it.

My work centres on security architecture, threat modelling (STRIDE), risk assessment and identity — IAM, PAM and CIAM — across Azure, AWS and GCP environments, with Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk for monitoring, and SOAR and Python for automation. Before KION, I spent three years as an Infrastructure Specialist at UNSW and close to a decade in network engineering and architecture at Grameenphone in Bangladesh.

I frame security decisions in terms the business can act on: classify the data first, apply least-privilege and Zero Trust controls, and where risk remains, document it with a named owner for formal acceptance.
